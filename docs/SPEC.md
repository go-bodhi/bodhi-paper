# Bodhi Paper: Product Spec

## 1. What it is
Bodhi Paper is a writing app for thinking. You write to get your thoughts
out of your head and clarify them, and the app keeps what you wrote.

## 2. Who it's for
Primary user: someone who writes to sort out their own thinking
(e.g. a student working through a problem). Anyone can use it, but
decisions are made for this person.

## 3. Principles
- Local-first: all data stays on the user's device.
- No cloud, no account, no network required.
- Writing comes first: nothing gets between the user and a blank page.
- Never lose the user's writing (autosave). 

## 4. In scope (v1)
1. Writing types: create, edit, delete types (e.g. thoughts, essays,
   notes, journal).
2. Writings: create, edit, delete writings inside each type.
3. Autosave while writing. 
4. Color themes: user picks one of several; the choice persists.
   (you said "multiple": I read it as choose one, not combine)
5. Search across all writings by text.
6. Filter search results by time range: week, month, year, all time,
   or a specific date.

## 5. Out of scope (v1)
- Sync between devices (later feature).
- Accounts, login, any cloud storage.
- Export/import. 
- Rich formatting beyond plain text/Markdown. 
- AI features. 

## 6. Non-goals
- Not a team or collaboration tool.
- Not a task manager or calendar.
